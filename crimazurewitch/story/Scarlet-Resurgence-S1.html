Lucy continues to walk through the makeshift hallway of mirrors. She glances at each reflection with a sense of melancholy.
Every Lucy she had come across had something special about herself, it made her look back at her own life.
Sure, she was a helpful soul. A friend who wanted to heal others, even when she herself was bleeding.
Someone that wished to someday be a great artist.
Hell, she was able to go from being unable to use Sen to being able to form three rings in the span of a month!
There were a lot of things she could take pride in…
She could feel her body melting in the fire. Slowly but surely, she was beginning to lose sight of where Lucille was headed.
Nevertheless, Lucy continued onward, the field of flames getting more scarce as she strayed from the “center”.
The threads of the worlds she peered into converged at one point, leaving behind a small, silky ribbon.

huh…? What's… this?
