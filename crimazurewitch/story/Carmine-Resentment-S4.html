Project

“Fracture Ray”

The mechanism which shall bring Lucy back to the normal, living world.
A stationary railcannon that resonates with I/O flow taken from the SEKAI itself in order to create a beacon of return.
It took days, if not weeks to fully engineer the cannon from scratch, let alone gathering the materials and assembling the damn contraption.

To power it, one must first synchronize the weapon with the SEKAI’s Untitled.
Following that, the railcannon must be aimed and prepared to fire.
After firing, the accumulation phase will begin, during which, small bits of information (Memory Fragments) will be scattered around the main beacon.
The beacon will only be stable enough to form upon collecting 40% of the total Memory Fragments, however it is advised to collect as many as possible before interacting with the main beam.

Multiple tests have been conducted beforehand, with most of them unfortunately yielding mediocre or poor results.
During one of the earlier trial runs, a nearby part of Heliopolis was engulfed in a bright light, leaving behind nothing but carmine roses and butterflies which quickly perished in the ruthless environment.
Soon after the incident, sights were set on the blood red ocean — Aputra.
The tests which were conducted after the decision to change targets offered a less destructive and more stable result.
